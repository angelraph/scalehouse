# Get paid (or pay someone) with Scalehouse: a 5-minute guide

Scalehouse pays people for real work or real deliveries, in USDC, on the Arc network. An AI agent sends the money, but a contract makes sure it can only pay **the right person, the right amount, once**.

You'll need two people:

- **The payer**: whoever owes the money (a shop owner, a client, a project).
- **The worker**: whoever did the job or delivered the goods.

The worker never has to pay anything or sign anything to receive money.

---

## If you're the WORKER (getting paid)

1. Install a crypto wallet app: **MetaMask**, **OKX Wallet** or **Rabby**.
2. Copy your wallet address (it starts with `0x…`).
3. Send it to the payer, along with what you did ("logo design", "10 loaves of bread").
4. That's it. When they confirm the job, the USDC lands in your wallet, usually within a minute. You'll get a receipt link anyone can check.

---

## If you're the PAYER (paying someone)

### Before you start
- Have a wallet (MetaMask, OKX Wallet or Rabby) with **at least 2 USDC on Arc**.
  - Don't have USDC on Arc yet? Ask the person who sent you this guide; we'll help.
- Find one other person to be your **confirmer** (a friend, teammate or staff member). They just check the work and tap once. Free.

### Step 1: Open your business ("house"), once
1. Go to **https://scalehouse-app.vercel.app** and tap **Connect**. Pick your wallet.
2. Tap **Open a house** and answer three questions:
   - **Your business name**
   - **Who records the work?** Choose **Me**.
   - **Who confirms it?** Choose **Someone else** and paste your confirmer's wallet.
   - **What do you pay for?** For example `Logo design`, price `1`.
3. Tap **Open the house** and approve in your wallet. It costs a few cents in fees.

### Step 2: Record the job
1. On your house page, tap **Cut a ticket**.
2. Tip: tap **Take or choose photo** and snap the invoice or receipt. The AI fills in the form for you and warns you if anything looks wrong.
3. Check the **wallet of the person you're paying**. It gets locked and can't be changed.
4. Tap **Cut the ticket** and approve.

### Step 3: Send it to your confirmer
1. On the ticket page, tap **Share on WhatsApp**.
2. Your confirmer opens the link, connects **their** wallet, types the quantity they checked, and taps **Sign my count**. Free.

### Step 4: Done
The agent pays within about a minute. The ticket page shows **Paid**, the amount, and why the agent paid it. Share that page with the worker as their receipt.

---

## Questions

- **Can it pay the wrong person?** No. The wallet is locked when the ticket is made. If anything tries to change it, the payment is frozen.
- **Can it pay twice?** No. Each ticket pays once, and the same invoice can't be used twice.
- **Where's my money before I pay?** In your own wallet. Scalehouse never holds it.
- **Is it public?** Yes. Tickets are on a public board, so put no personal details in notes or photos.
